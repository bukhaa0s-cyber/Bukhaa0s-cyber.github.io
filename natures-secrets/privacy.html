Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the "Nature's Secrets" channel on YouTube and TikTok and "Animal Shorts", the private publishing tool the channel owner uses to upload the channel's own videos.

Who uses the tool

Animal Shorts is not a public app. It is used only by the channel owner, to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. No other person signs in to it.

What Google data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel and set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails;
  • add those videos to the channel's own playlists;
  • post a comment from the channel on its own videos;
  • read the channel's own name and profile picture to brand the videos.

The tool does not read, collect or store any data about viewers, subscribers or other YouTube users, and it does not access any other Google service.

What TikTok data the tool accesses

With the account owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit), the tool uses the TikTok Content Posting API to:

  • read the account's basic profile (display name and avatar) and its posting settings, such as the allowed privacy options and maximum video length;
  • upload and publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account with their captions and settings.

The tool does not read, collect or store data about other TikTok users, followers or viewers.

How data is stored and shared

The only credentials kept are the channel owner's own access tokens, stored encrypted as private secrets in the owner's GitHub account and used only to publish to the owner's accounts. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Watching our videos

When you watch or comment on our videos, your data is handled by the platform under the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy and the TikTok Privacy Policy. We only see the anonymous, aggregated statistics each platform shows every creator.

Revoking access

Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time at Google security settings and in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. Data deletion requests can be sent to the contact below and are handled within 30 days.
